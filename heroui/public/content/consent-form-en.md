# Personal Data Protection Act Notice and Consent Form

## 1. Organization
Buddhist Tzu Chi Foundation

## 2. Purpose of Collection
To process the distribution of disaster relief funds in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act.

## 3. Categories of Personal Data
Name, ID number, contact phone, residential address, bank account information, ID document images, bank account document images, signature.

## 4. Period, Region, Recipients and Methods of Use
1. **Period**: Data will be retained for five years after completion of the distribution process.
2. **Region**: Within the territory of the Republic of China.
3. **Recipients**: Tzu Chi Foundation and commissioned financial institutions.
4. **Methods**: Written documents, electronic files, internet transmission, etc.

## 5. Your Rights
Under the Personal Data Protection Act, you may exercise the following rights:
1. Query or request access to your personal data.
2. Request copies of your personal data.
3. Request correction or supplementation of your personal data.
4. Request cessation of collection, processing or use of your personal data.
5. Request deletion of your personal data.

## 6. Consequences of Not Providing Personal Data
If you do not agree to provide personal data, we will be unable to process the relief fund distribution.
