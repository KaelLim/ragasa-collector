# Comfort Letter

Dear Disaster-Affected Friend,

Thank you for maintaining your strength and resilience during this difficult time.

The Tzu Chi Foundation understands that while natural disasters are merciless, human compassion prevails. During your time of need, we extend our helping hand and offer emergency relief funds to ease your burden and accompany you through this challenging period.

To ensure the relief funds are transferred to your account quickly and accurately, we kindly ask for your cooperation in providing the necessary information. We promise that all personal information you provide will be used solely for this relief fund distribution and will be kept strictly confidential.

May you rebuild your home soon and return to a normal life.

With best wishes for your peace and health,

**Tzu Chi Foundation**
**Master Cheng Yen**
